## Full Description

### Main Description

Transform your small farm or homestead with FarmAssist – the intelligent farm management app designed specifically for farms under 100 acres.

**COMPREHENSIVE CROP MANAGEMENT**
• Track 100+ crops with detailed growing requirements
• Monitor planting and harvest dates
• Organize fields and beds with size tracking
• Access complete growing guides (light, water, soil, pH, spacing)
• Plan cover crops for soil health

**SMART TASK MANAGEMENT**
• AI-generated task suggestions based on crop stages
• Priority system (low, medium, high)
• Track watering, fertilizing, weeding, pest control, and more
• Never miss important farming activities
• Mark tasks complete with timestamps

**INVENTORY CONTROL**
• Manage seeds, fertilizers, and packaging
• Track storage locations and capacity
• Monitor transplant inventory
• Get low stock alerts
• Organize all your farm supplies in one place

**REVENUE TRACKING** (Pro)
• Record income by crop and sales channel
• Track expenses by category
• Generate detailed financial reports
• Monitor profitability by crop
• Analyze sales channels (roadside, restaurant, CSA, farmers market)
• Export data for tax purposes

**EQUIPMENT MANAGEMENT**
• Track all farm equipment
• Log maintenance and service history
• Monitor equipment hours
• Record costs and repairs
• Set maintenance reminders

**AI-POWERED INSIGHTS**
• Chat with AI for farming advice
• Get context-aware recommendations
• Upload photos for plant problem diagnosis
• Receive weather-based task suggestions (Pro)
• Access 7-day weather forecasts with AI analysis (Pro)

**MARKETPLACE**
• Buy, sell, or rent farm equipment
• Sell produce directly to customers
• Built-in messaging system
• Seller ratings and reviews
• Location-based search
• Secure transactions

**DESIGNED FOR SMALL FARMS**
• Works in low-service rural areas
• Intuitive interface for all skill levels
• Supports self-sufficiency and commercial operations
• Roadside stands, restaurants, CSAs, farmers markets
• Both metric and imperial units

**FREE FEATURES**
✓ Basic crop management
✓ Task tracking
✓ Inventory management
✓ Equipment tracking
✓ Basic AI assistant
✓ Marketplace access

**PRO FEATURES** ($9.99/month or $99/year)
✓ All free features
✓ Advanced AI (weather insights, problem diagnosis)
✓ Detailed revenue reports
✓ Unlimited marketplace listings
✓ Priority support
✓ Export capabilities

Whether you're growing vegetables, fruits, flowers, herbs, or spices, FarmAssist helps you stay organized, make informed decisions, and maximize your farm's potential.

**PERFECT FOR:**
• Small farms (under 100 acres)
• Homesteaders
• Market gardeners
• CSA operators
• Organic farmers
• Hobby farmers
• Urban farmers
